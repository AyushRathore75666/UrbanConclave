Grand Innovation Challenge

The Grand Innovation Challenge seeks technology-enabled, scalable, interoperable and field-deployable solutions that can help authorities transition from predominantly reactive management to predictive, preventive and real-time decision making.

The challenge is organised under four interconnected problem areas. Participants are encouraged to submit proposals that comprehensively address one of these areas.

Mobility & Traffic Management

During Simhastha, Ujjain will experience significant and rapidly changing movement of pilgrims, residents, vehicles and pedestrians. Traffic conditions can shift quickly due to religious events, bathing schedules, peak travel periods, weather and crowd density.

At such times, temporary traffic arrangements such as diversions, pedestrian corridors, parking zones and emergency routes are needed.

The challenge is to develop solutions that help authorities monitor, predict and manage mobility across the Mela area in real time.

Applicants should demonstrate capabilities in technology, urban planning and/or transport planning. Proposals may address one or more of the challenges.

Track 1 · Mobility and Traffic Management

Crowd Management & Pilgrim Safety

Simhastha will bring together large numbers of people across ghats, temples, transit hubs, marketplaces, accommodation areas and public spaces. Crowd conditions can change rapidly due to religious events, weather, transport arrivals and movement patterns.

Authorities need solutions that provide real-time situational awareness, help anticipate risks and support timely interventions while preserving the safety, dignity and experience of pilgrims.

Applicants should demonstrate capabilities in technology, urban design and planning, and/or behavioural science. Proposals may address one or more of the challenges.

Track 2 · Crowd Management and Pilgrim Safety

Sanitation & Cleanliness Management

The large and temporary nature of Simhastha will create significant demand for waste management, sanitation services and public hygiene infrastructure.

Waste generation and the use of sanitation facilities will vary across locations and time, requiring a responsive and data-driven approach to service delivery.

The challenge is to develop solutions that help authorities maintain cleanliness, improve operational efficiency and ensure reliable sanitation services throughout the event.

Applicants should demonstrate capabilities in technology and/or planning. Proposals may address one or more of the challenges.

Track 3 · Sanitation and Cleanliness Management

Supply Chain Management & Logistics

Simhastha will function as a temporary city, requiring the timely delivery of food, drinking water, medical supplies, sanitation materials, fuel and other essential goods across a large and dynamic event area.

The challenge is to ensure reliable supply while minimizing congestion, stock shortages and unnecessary vehicle movement.

Authorities need solutions that can help forecast demand, manage inventories and coordinate logistics efficiently in a rapidly changing environment.

Applicants should demonstrate capabilities in technology and/or supply chain and logistics planning. Proposals may address one or more of the challenges.

Track 4 · Supply Chain Management and Logistics
