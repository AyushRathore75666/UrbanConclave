About

Department of Urban Development And Housing

The Department of Urban Development And Housing, Government of Madhya Pradesh, leads urban development initiatives across the state. The Madhya Pradesh Urban Growth Conclave 2.0 is its flagship urban investment and policy platform and a key milestone on the Road to GIS 2027.

About the Conclave 2.0

The Madhya Pradesh Urban Growth Conclave 2.0 will showcase major urban projects, policy reforms, investment opportunities, MoU's, infrastructure initiatives and future-ready urban development strategies.

Event agenda

28th October 2026 at the Brilliant Convention Centre, Indore. Panel discussions begin at 2:00 PM, after the plenary, HCM meetings and networking lunch.

  1. 11:45 AM

    Plenary Session

    Until 1:30 PM. Welcome address, urban growth presentation, film presentation, industry perspective, keynote session, minister address, Chief Minister address, policy launches, investment announcements, MoU exchange and expo launch.

  2. 1:30 PM

    HCM One-to-One Meetings

    Until 2:00 PM. Meeting requests are reviewed and shortlisted by the organising committee. Submission of a request does not guarantee an appointment.

  3. 2:00 PM

    Networking Lunch

    Lunch before the panel discussions.

  4. 2:00 PM

    Panel Discussions

    From 2:00 PM onwards. Beyond Metros; Reimagining Urban Governance; Building Climate-Smart Cities; Urban Financing and Investment in Madhya Pradesh; Simhastha; Solar; Hackathon; Meet one to one leadership.
